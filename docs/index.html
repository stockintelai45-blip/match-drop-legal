Match Drop

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Short version: Match Drop does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, and no analytics or tracking of any kind. The only data the app keeps is your score and a couple of settings, stored locally on your own device.

What data Match Drop collects

None. Match Drop is a self-contained, offline puzzle game. It does not:

  • require or support user accounts, sign-in, or sign-up;
  • show advertisements;
  • include any analytics, crash-reporting, or tracking SDK;
  • send any gameplay data, device information, or identifiers to any server;
  • use cookies or any cross-app/cross-site tracking technology.

What's stored on your device

Match Drop saves a small amount of data using your device's local storage (localStorage), purely so the game remembers things between sessions. This data never leaves your device:

  • matchdrop_best — your best score so far.
  • matchdrop_prefs — your preferences, such as whether sound and haptics are turned on, and whether you've seen the in-game tutorial.

You can clear this data at any time by clearing the app's storage/data in your device settings, or by uninstalling the app.

Sharing your score

Match Drop includes an optional "Share" feature that lets you generate an image of your score and share it using your device's native share sheet (for example, to Messages or social media), if you choose to use it. This image is generated entirely on your device. Match Drop itself does not send this image, your score, or any other data to us or to any third party — what happens to the image after you hand it to the share sheet depends on which app you choose to share it with, and is covered by that app's own privacy policy.

Third-party services

Match Drop does not integrate any third-party advertising, analytics, backend, or over-the-air update service. There is nothing in the app that contacts an external server.

Children's privacy

Because Match Drop collects no personal data from anyone, including children, there is nothing to disclose or restrict with respect to children's privacy. The game contains no chat, no user-generated content, and no way for players to contact one another.

Changes to this policy

If Match Drop's data practices ever change (for example, if ads or an update/sync service are added in a future version), this policy will be updated to accurately describe exactly what is collected and why, before that version is released.
