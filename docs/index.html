Boinkle

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 6, 2026

Summary: Boinkle does not require an account and does not collect any personal details from you. The game stores your best score, level progress, and sound and haptics settings only on your own device. Boinkle shows ads served by Google AdMob, which may collect device identifiers, IP address, approximate location, and ad interaction data to show and measure ads. Personalised ads only run with your consent where required by law (EU/EEA/UK), and on iOS only if you allow tracking in the system prompt. You can change your ad choices at any time in the app's Settings. If in-app purchases are added, they are handled by Apple, Google, and RevenueCat — we never see your payment details. We do not sell your data.

Who we are

Boinkle is made by Bubble Apps (developer: Andreas Achillea). If you have questions about this policy or how your data is handled, contact us at YOUR-EMAIL-HERE.

What Boinkle stores on your device

Boinkle has no accounts and does not ask you for any personal details. The following is saved locally on your device only, and is never sent to us:

  • your best score;
  • your level progress;
  • your sound and haptics settings.

You can clear this at any time by clearing the app's storage/data in your device settings, or by uninstalling the app.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Boinkle shows ads through Google AdMob. Ads are served by Google, and showing them may involve Google and its partners collecting information such as:

  • device identifiers (such as your device's advertising identifier);
  • your IP address and approximate location;
  • ad interaction data, such as which ads you see, tap, or dismiss.

Where the law requires it (in the EU, EEA, and UK), we only show personalised ads if you give consent through the in-app consent prompt. On iOS, personalised ads and identifier-based tracking only happen if you allow tracking when asked by Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you don't consent, or don't allow tracking, you'll still see ads, but they'll be less personalised.

You can change your ad consent choice at any time in-app: Settings > Privacy settings. On iPhone, you can also review or change tracking permission at any time under Privacy & Security > Tracking in your device's Settings app.

For more detail on what Google collects and how it's used, see Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

In-app purchases

Boinkle may add optional in-app purchases in a future update (for example, to remove ads). Purchases are handled entirely by Apple's App Store or Google Play, together with RevenueCat, which we use to manage entitlements. Your payment details (card numbers, billing information) are handled by Apple or Google and never reach us or RevenueCat.

No sale of data

We do not sell your personal data to anyone.

Children's privacy

Boinkle is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us at YOUR-EMAIL-HERE and we will address it.

Data retention

Data stored on your device (best score, level progress, settings) stays there until you clear it or uninstall the app. We don't hold a copy of it on our servers. Advertising data collected by Google is retained and processed under Google's own privacy policy.

Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational steps to protect any data processed in connection with Boinkle. No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure, but we work to use practices appropriate to the limited data involved.

International transfers

Advertising partners such as Google may process data outside your country, including in the United States. Where this involves transfers from the EU, EEA, or UK, Google relies on appropriate safeguards, such as Standard Contractual Clauses, as described in Google's own privacy policy.
