Frequently asked questions

How do I reset my progress?

Boinkle keeps your best score, level progress, and settings only on your own device — there's no account to delete. To reset everything, clear the app's data or storage from your device's Settings app (under the app's own storage/usage settings), or uninstall and reinstall Boinkle.

How do I turn off sound and haptics?

Tap the speaker icon in the game's bottom toolbar to mute or unmute sound. Haptics (vibration feedback) follows your device's system haptics/vibration setting — turn it off from your device's own Settings if you'd prefer the game not to vibrate.

How do I change my ad consent?

Open Settings > Privacy settings inside Boinkle to review or change your advertising consent choices at any time. On iPhone, you can also review tracking permission under Privacy & Security > Tracking in your device's Settings app.

How do I restore purchases?

Boinkle doesn't currently offer any in-app purchases. If and when a "Remove Ads" or similar purchase is added, a "Restore Purchases" option will appear in Settings so you can restore it on a new device without paying again.

How do I report a bug?

Email YOUR-EMAIL-HERE with a short description of what happened, what level/screen you were on, and your device model and OS version if you know it. A screenshot or screen recording helps a lot.
